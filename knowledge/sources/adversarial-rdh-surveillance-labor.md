# Adversarial — burned RDH: Smile Notes as surveillance / unpaid documentation labor

- **Type**: red-team / adversarial stakeholder simulation (single lens, first person)
- **Ingested**: 2026-08-08
- **Tags**: ux, adversarial, hygienist, labor, surveillance, digest, gpa, andon, adoption, red-team
- **Status**: worker-protective backlog + walkout kill list; not live interviews
- **Method**: One mock agent instructed to **hate** Smile Notes as a production-management tool disguised as patient safety. Personality: mid-career RDH, Dentrix → Open Dental → Curve survivor, union-skeptic (not unionized — just done being graded). Grounded in shipped surfaces (digest, GPA/ring, Andons, Lead coaching, Check-your-note, paste handoff). **Not** observed Cornerstone sessions.

## Axiom

You did not hire me to write essays for your malpractice carrier. You hired me to clean teeth on a packed schedule. Smile Notes that grades me, digests me, Andons me, and coaches me through a Lead who has never scaled my column is **unpaid documentation labor with a dashboard**.

**Why it matters:** Hygienists kill pilots with quiet bypass (Curve QuickText only) or loud walkouts. Friendly panels ask for prettier chips. This panel names the **surveillance smell** and the two features that end the conversation.

## Six hates

### 1. The digest — homework about me, paid to someone else

Lead opens a page that names people. Patterns, “signals,” density, coverage. You swear it is not a scoreboard. I am not stupid. Anything that lists my name next to a defect rate is a **performance file with better fonts**. I already charted the patient. Now my unpaid evening is someone else’s coaching agenda. If the digest exists, I assume every thin note is a strike.

### 2. GPA / ProgressRing — school for adults with ultrasonic in hand

A letter grade on a clinical note is contempt. You freeze GPA at filing like that makes it sacred. I see a ring that implies I am behind while blood is in the sulcus and the front desk is seating the next patient. Grade theater does not make anesthetic amounts appear; it makes me hate the tool that is timing my competence. If a thin note can still look like an “A,” you are lying. If it cannot, you are punishing speed. Either way I am the sucker.

### 3. Andons — public shame lights for private work

Unset-role amber as my greeting. Scope chips. Waiting-for-dentist banners. Ready that is not ready. An Andon is a factory floor invention: stop the line, expose the worker. I am not a Toyota cell. I am one hygienist with gloves on and a schedule that does not care about your honesty doctrine. Every chip that broadcasts “incomplete” to whoever walks past the iPad is a **status humiliation ritual**. Fix the blocker. Do not decorate my failure.

### 4. Lead coaching — sermons from people who do not sit my column

Byte tips. Check-your-note sermons. Digest → “coach this week.” Lead who files zero prophies telling me my soft tissue language is thin. Coaching that cannot insert the missing line in one thumb tap is **moralizing my throughput**. I do not need a personality intervention. I need the note path to be as fast as Favorites without inventing findings. If coaching requires a meeting, you stole production.

### 5. Two-app paste tax — you moved liability onto my break

Charter says no Curve write-back. Fine. Then every hop is my unpaid labor: build here, Copy, pray the clipboard holds, paste there, clean formatting, attach the visit, smile at the patient who waited. You call it risk reduction. I call it **offloading carrier anxiety onto hygiene minutes**. If paste fidelity fails once, I am done pretending this is my job.

### 6. Attestation / checkbox theater — force me to swear so you can sleep

Long attest lists. “Correct as written.” Killer items that nag then let Copy through anyway — or block Copy after I already ran late. You want my name on a checkbox so the deposition has a warm body. That is not safety. That is **liability laundering**. Independent verification that pulls a second clinician off the floor during a hygiene pileup is the same theft with a policy sticker.

## Four worker-protective fixes (ship these or admit you are managing people)

Effort = invasiveness. These are not “delight.” They are **anti-surveillance rails** so hygiene does not treat Smile Notes as HR.

| # | Fix | What it protects | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **W1** | **Hygiene-facing path: no GPA, no ring-as-grade, no personal digest rows** | Dignity / wage minutes | Lead-only digest stays Lead-only. Builder never implies a school grade. Bind any completeness UI to open killers only — never a letter. If you need a number, keep it off the chairside surface forever. |
| **W2** | **One-thumb finish: blocker → insert → Copy; zero sermon** | Throughput | Glove-first chips, ≥44px, Next names the single blocker. Check-your-note collapses to ≤3 litigation killers on hygiene handoff. Full AuditPanel is opt-in, never the finish path. |
| **W3** | **Practice-level coaching only; no named RDH on the floor** | Anti-scoreboard | Digest → 90-second *practice* coach card (templates/vocab/packs). Person-scoped signals never become huddle entertainment, screenshots, or “growth plans.” Breach of this = trust death. |
| **W4** | **Paid documentation time or schedule credit — product must assume unpaid minutes are theft** | Labor honesty | Not a UI widget: owner policy. If Smile Notes adds median ≥X minutes vs Curve QuickText, the pilot dies unless chairs are cut or paid doc blocks exist. Instrument the delta; do not gaslight. |

## Two features that cause a walkout

Ship either of these and hygiene stops cooperating. Quietly first. Then loudly.

| # | Walkout feature | Why it ends the room |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **X1** | **Peer scoreboards / ranked GPA / “top hygienist” digest / public Andon walls** | That is performance management with clinical cosplay. People will chart the shortest gate-clearing note, refuse the second app, or leave. Your own digest code already knows this — do not betray it with “engagement.” |
| **X2** | **Forced always-on ambient / silent AI fill that invents findings, or mandatory after-hours chart completion tracked to the person** | Invented soft tissue = she loses the only reason to respect you, and she will not sign her license under a ghostwriter. Mandated unpaid catch-up queues = wage theft with a login. Either is a resignation letter with better vocabulary. |

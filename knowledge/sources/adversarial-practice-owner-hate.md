# Adversarial hate — practice owner who writes the check

- **Type**: red-team / adversarial buyer review (not a live owner interview)
- **Ingested**: 2026-08-08
- **Tags**: practice-owner, roi, seat-cost, training, chairside-time, claim-defensibility, red-team, economics, pilot-gates
- **Status**: decision gates ready; no lawsuit-prevention claim allowed in any pitch
- **Method**: Hostile mock buyer — Tennessee multi-office owner who signs payroll and software — instructed to **hate** Smile Notes until the spreadsheet closes. Grounded in Cornerstone scale (~30 staff, 3 offices, ~$6.5M revenue estimate), claim-file epistemic frame (`litigation-documentation-research.md`), and product reality (paste into Curve; deterministic gates; AI optional). **Not** observed P&L — working thresholds the owner would demand before writing another check.

## Axiom

I do not buy “safer notes.” I buy **minutes back**, **OT not spent**, and **gaps closed that I can count**. If you cannot put a number next to seat cost, training hours, doctor slowdown, and a measurable documentation outcome — you are selling me anxiety with a login.

**Why it matters:** The Doctors Company found documentation issues in **19%** of 1,185 closed dental claims. That is a risk **pattern**, not a product ROI. This codebase’s own litigation digest says lawsuit prevention is a **negative proof** — unprovable. Any pitch that pretends otherwise is a trap I will kill in the first five minutes.

## The trap — defensibility theater

**“Carrier research shows documentation gaps → buy Smile Notes → fewer claims”** is the trap.

It sounds adult. It cites The Doctors Company **19%** and the findings / consent / rationale stack. The app really does encode those gaps as rules. Screenshots look like risk management.

Then the invoice hits: seats × writers; a training afternoon; doctors 90 seconds slower; staff paste into Curve and thin the note anyway; and six months later nobody can show that open killer gaps at export moved — only that “we take documentation seriously.”

That is **premium stacking**: malpractice premium + software premium + OT + production drag, with the ROI line left blank because lawsuit prevention is unprovable and you never instrumented the only measurable proxy (gap closure under a time guardrail).

Do not sell me that. Sell me a **90-day pilot** with shared-device pricing, a training budget in hours not vibes, a doctor-time stop-loss, and a gap metric. Fail the numbers → kill the seats. Pass → then talk about year two.

## Explicitly do not ship (owner hate)

- Lawsuit-prevention, “board-proof,” or indemnity-ROI claims in UI, sales, or `/risk` copy.
- Per-seat quotes that ignore shared operatory tablets.
- Mandatory multi-hour training as the adoption plan.
- Features that raise median note time without a published stop-loss.
- Peer quality scoreboards.
- Ambient AI as the time-savings story.
